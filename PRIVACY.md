# Tab Volume Booster Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy describes data handling by Tab Volume Booster version 2.0.4.

## Information processed locally

Website audio: The extension processes audio from supported website audio/video players in your browser to apply volume, equalizer, stereo balance, leveling, and peak protection. It does not make or retain audio recordings, upload audio, or send audio to the developer or third parties.

Website addresses: The extension reads tab and media addresses to identify websites, select the correct per-site audio settings, and determine whether a player can be processed. Website hostnames are stored locally as keys for saved per-site preferences. The extension does not maintain a chronological log of visited pages or visit times and does not transmit website addresses.

Preferences and temporary player state: The extension stores volume, EQ, balance, audio-processing choices, custom presets, themes, and save-scope preferences in local browser storage. Session storage holds temporary tab settings and identifiers for connected tabs, frames, and players.

## How information is used

Information is used only to provide the extension's audio controls, remember your preferences, and apply those preferences to the correct players. The extension does not collect account credentials, payment information, contact details, location information, or interaction logs for analytics. It has no advertising, tracking, or analytics integrations and makes no network requests of its own.

## Sharing and limited use

The extension does not sell or transfer the information it handles to third parties. It does not use that information for advertising, unrelated purposes, creditworthiness decisions, or lending. The developer does not receive or have remote access to the extension's locally handled data.

Tab Volume Booster's use of user data adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Retention and deletion

Saved preferences remain locally until you change them or use Clear All Saved Settings in the extension. That button deletes saved audio and theme preferences. Temporary per-tab settings and player registrations are removed when the corresponding tab closes or the browser session ends. Audio samples are processed transiently for playback and are not saved as recordings.

## Privacy questions

Use the publisher's support/contact information on the Tab Volume Booster Chrome Web Store listing for questions about this policy.
